# EXP-101 Gift panel redesign - experiment readout

## Summary
Experiment EXP-101 (Gift panel redesign, key gift_panel_v2) was run by LIVE Monetisation on 120,000 users for 14 days. Decision: Ship. Gifts sent per viewer up +4.7% (95% CI +2.1% to +7.4%); all guardrails healthy.

## Hypothesis
A larger, scrollable gift panel with recent gifts pinned will make gifting easier and increase gifts per viewer.

## Sample ratio check
Treatment received 50.08% of users (chi-square p = 0.58). The split is healthy.

## Metric results
Gifts sent per viewer (primary): +4.7% relative change, 95% CI +2.1% to +7.4%, adjusted p = 0.000597.
Gift value per viewer (secondary): +5.2% relative change, 95% CI +2.4% to +8.0%, adjusted p = 0.000571.
Gifts per watch hour (secondary): +5.7% relative change, 95% CI +3.9% to +7.5%, adjusted p = 2.22e-09.
LIVE watch minutes per viewer (secondary): -1.1% relative change, 95% CI -2.4% to +0.1%, adjusted p = 0.123. CUPED removed 46% of the variance.
Report rate (guardrail): -1.9% relative change, 95% CI -7.5% to +3.7%, adjusted p = 0.503.
7-day retention (guardrail): -0.4% relative change, 95% CI -1.2% to +0.5%, adjusted p = 0.444.

## Segments
Significant segment effects on the primary metric: country BR +10.1%; device ios +8.2%; is_new False +4.3%.

## Peeking
The cumulative p-value first dropped below 0.05 on day 1.

# Metric dictionary

## LIVE watch minutes per viewer
Metric key watch_min. Total minutes of LIVE content watched per user during the experiment window. Unit: min. Higher is better.

## Gifts sent per viewer
Metric key gifts. Number of virtual gifts a user sent to creators during the window. Unit: gifts. Higher is better.

## Gift value per viewer
Metric key gift_value. Coins spent on gifts per user; the main monetisation metric for LIVE. Unit: coins. Higher is better.

## Gifts per watch hour
Metric key gifts_per_hour. Gifts divided by hours watched, pooled per arm. A ratio metric, so its variance uses the delta method. Unit: gifts/h. Higher is better.

## Report rate
Metric key report_rate. Share of users who reported a LIVE stream as harmful or annoying. A guardrail: it must not rise. Unit: %. Lower is better.

## 7-day retention
Metric key d7_retention. Share of users who came back to LIVE in the 7 days after the window. A guardrail: it must not fall. Unit: %. Higher is better.

# EXP-104 Beauty filter promotion for hosts - experiment readout

## Summary
Experiment EXP-104 (Beauty filter promotion for hosts, key beauty_filter_promo) was run by LIVE Creator Tools on 97,526 users for 14 days. Decision: Invalid - rerun. Sample ratio mismatch: treatment share 49.15% vs 50% expected (p = 1.3e-07). Results cannot be trusted until the assignment bug is fixed.

## Hypothesis
Promoting beauty filters to hosts will make streams more attractive and raise watch time.

## Sample ratio check
Treatment received 49.15% of users (chi-square p = 1.3e-07). This is a sample ratio mismatch, so the results are not trustworthy.

## Metric results
LIVE watch minutes per viewer (primary): +1.7% relative change, 95% CI +0.3% to +3.1%, adjusted p = 0.0806. CUPED removed 45% of the variance.
Gifts sent per viewer (secondary): +1.9% relative change, 95% CI -0.9% to +4.6%, adjusted p = 0.306.
Gift value per viewer (secondary): +1.1% relative change, 95% CI -1.8% to +4.0%, adjusted p = 0.561.
Report rate (guardrail): +0.1% relative change, 95% CI -6.3% to +6.6%, adjusted p = 0.966.
7-day retention (guardrail): +0.9% relative change, 95% CI +0.0% to +1.9%, adjusted p = 0.115.

## Segments
Significant segment effects on the primary metric: device android_low +3.3%.

## Peeking
The cumulative p-value first dropped below 0.05 on day 1.

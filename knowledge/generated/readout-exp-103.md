# EXP-103 ML ranking for LIVE comments - experiment readout

## Summary
Experiment EXP-103 (ML ranking for LIVE comments, key comment_ranking_ml) was run by LIVE Interaction on 100,000 users for 14 days. Decision: No clear effect. LIVE watch minutes per viewer moved -0.2% but the 95% CI (-1.6% to +1.2%) includes zero. Keep control or iterate.

## Hypothesis
Ranking comments with an engagement model will make rooms feel livelier and raise watch time.

## Sample ratio check
Treatment received 49.87% of users (chi-square p = 0.4). The split is healthy.

## Metric results
LIVE watch minutes per viewer (primary): -0.2% relative change, 95% CI -1.6% to +1.2%, adjusted p = 0.956. CUPED removed 46% of the variance.
Gifts sent per viewer (secondary): +1.0% relative change, 95% CI -1.7% to +3.6%, adjusted p = 0.956.
Gift value per viewer (secondary): +0.1% relative change, 95% CI -2.7% to +2.9%, adjusted p = 0.956.
Report rate (guardrail): +4.6% relative change, 95% CI -1.9% to +11.2%, adjusted p = 0.786.
7-day retention (guardrail): +0.1% relative change, 95% CI -0.9% to +1.0%, adjusted p = 0.956.

## Segments
No segment showed a significant effect on the primary metric after multiple-testing correction.

## Peeking
The cumulative p-value never dropped below 0.05 during the test.
